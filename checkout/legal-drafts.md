# Lyndco Systems Ltd — compliance drafting pack (NOT for publication)

**Company information to insert:** legal name, number, registered office, contact address, support email, VAT status, payment processor, data processors, retention schedule, complaint process, support commitment, release/version policy.

## Checkout consent text — separate mandatory unchecked controls
1. "I agree to the Terms of Sale and Software Licence." [link to accessible versions]
2. "I expressly request immediate supply of the digital software before the 14-day cancellation period ends."
3. "I understand that once digital supply begins with my consent, I will lose the 14-day right to cancel for change of mind. My statutory rights if digital content is faulty remain unaffected."
Record consent, terms version, transaction, time and confirmation in durable records. Do not begin download or activation unless required consent is captured and confirmed. Require the explicit purchase total and obligation-to-pay button. Provide durable confirmation of the contract.

## Marketing
Separate unchecked optional box: "Email me about other Lyndco business tools. I can unsubscribe at any time." Track timestamp, language/version, and an unsubscribe/suppression mechanism. Market research interest checkboxes are separate and optional.

## Privacy notice — required sections
Controller identity and contact details; data categories (contact, order, payment reference, licence devices, marketing preference, optional interests, technical logs); purposes and UK GDPR lawful bases; recipients including Stripe, Supabase, GitHub and email platform subject to actual architecture; international transfers with safeguards; retention by category; rights and complaint route to ICO; cookies/storage audit and correct PECR controls; security incident contact. Do not claim no servers store personal data: customer estimates are local, but purchase/licence records are server side.

## Software Licence — points for solicitor
Non-exclusive, non-transferable single-user licence, paid team seats, permitted device replacements and offline limitations, intellectual property, restrictions on redistribution, term/termination, security and backup responsibilities, support scope and version compatibility, third-party app availability, acceptable use, statutory rights preserved, fair limits on liability (no blanket exclusion of liability for negligence causing death/injury or fraud), dispute/complaint process.

## Product and refund terms
Distinguish indicative estimates from binding quotations. Explain input assumptions and markup (not margin), VAT as calculator only, no financial/tax advice, and the user's obligation to verify pricing. Describe digital delivery, cancellation consent, digital-content conformity/remedies, refunds for faulty software, any optional paid technical support, security update policy and data export/backup.

## Review gates
- UK consumer and B2B terms review by qualified UK solicitor.
- Privacy/cookie analysis based on actual services deployed.
- ICO data protection fee self-assessment.
- Confirm software/product name clearance.
- Confirm VAT treatment, sales territory, digital supply and invoices with accountant.
- Confirm public company information, service address and disclosure requirements.
