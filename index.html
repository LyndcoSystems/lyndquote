Finish the job. Keep the connection.

HelpPoint gives your customer a convenient route back to your business long after you've packed away your tools.

01 / LEAVE IT

Place a sticker

Leave a discreet NFC sticker on suitable equipment, paperwork or another accessible surface.

02 / TOUCH IT

Customer taps

A compatible smartphone opens their contact options. No separate app required for standard NFC web links.

03 / CONNECT

You're within reach

Customers can use the contact routes you've configured, including phone, WhatsApp, email or your website.
